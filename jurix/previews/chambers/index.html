Article 8Limitation of Liability

§8.1Exclusion of Consequential Damages. Neither party shall be liable to the other for any indirect, incidental, special, or consequential damages, including loss of profits or goodwill, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, even if advised of the possibility of such damages.

§8.2Cap on Liability. Except for a party’s obligations under Section 10 (Confidentiality), Supplier’s aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the three months preceding the claim. The foregoing limitation applies whether a claim arises in contract, tort, or otherwise.
